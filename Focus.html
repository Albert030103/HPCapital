Home  >  Focus



We focus on disruptive innovations in the following three specific sectors:
  • Information and High Technology.
  • Life Science and Health Care
  • Clean Energy and Technology
We believe these sectors have great potential for high growth rates and value creation, and Canada holds great competitive advantages in these sectors.


Information and High Technology

This sector includes information, communications technology, AI, fintech, blockchain, VR, AR, autonomous transportation technologies, etc.



Life Science and Health Care

Many countries are facing aging populations. Improving quality of life and providing better healthcare is a priority for them. This creates tremendous demand for health care service, brings significant opportunities for enterprises and organizations in these fields. This sector includes: diagnostics, biopharmaceuticals, pharmaceuticals and medical devices companies, hospitals, other components of the health care system, etc.





Clean Energy and Technology

Environmental sustainability is a big issue facing today’s world. Canada is a global leader and pioneer in the clean energy and technology sector. This sector includes renewable energy (such as wind power, solar power, biofuel, etc.), and technology that can improve energy efficiency and reduce human's environmental impact.
